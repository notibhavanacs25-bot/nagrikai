Official Authentication Guide

NagrikAI explains documents, but only official state and central government portals can legally authenticate certificates. Here is where and how to verify this document:

1
Check Digital Barcode or QR Code

Official Indian certificates feature a 2D QR Code. Scan it using the official DigiLocker app or state scanner to see the digitally signed government payload.

2
Verify on Official Government Portal: State Portal

Enter the Reference Number shown on this document directly on the secured government (.gov.in or .nic.in) portal.

3
Cross-check with DigiLocker Repository

Legally recognized documents are available in your DigiLocker under the Information Technology (Preservation and Retention of Information by Intermediaries Providing Digital Locker Facilities) Rules, 2016.
