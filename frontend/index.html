Core Capabilities

Designed for Every Indian Citizen

Overcoming language barriers, complex bureaucratic terminology, and scattered portals with accessible AI.

1. Document Simplification

Upload any PDF, image, or notice. NagrikAI identifies the document type, clarifies why you received it, and highlights amounts, fees, and references in plain words.

Explore Simplifier

2. Validity & Deadline Detection

Never miss a response window or lose a subsidy. NagrikAI detects expiration dates, highlights urgent response deadlines, and exports reminders directly to your Google Calendar.

Check Deadlines

Know exactly how to authenticate your document on DigiLocker, Bhoomi, or Parivahan. AI explanations are clearly segregated from verified official government registries.
